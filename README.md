# infinite fusion - unsprited fusions finder

a little site that finds fusions in pokémon infinite fusion that don't have a custom sprite yet, so you can pick something to sprite.
i struggle with being inspired so something random helps me a lot. hope it helps you too :D

**use it here:** https://tullio-official.github.io/unsprited-fusions-finder/

## how it works

hit **go!** (or spacebar) and you get 6 random unsprited fusions. use the head and body filters to narrow it down by pokémon, type, size, region, color, body shape, evolution stage, legendaries, or pokémon that barely have any sprites.

the fusion number copies with one click, and dex opens the fusion on [infinitefusiondex.com](https://infinitefusiondex.com). filters stay in the page link, so you can share a search with someone.
